# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility
Full-time employees in good standing may request a hybrid or fully remote arrangement after 90 days of employment. Managers must approve schedules that maintain core collaboration hours of 10:00–15:00 local time.

## Equipment
The company provides a laptop and secure VPN for approved remote workers. Personal devices may not store customer PII or regulated data.

## Security
Remote workers must enable full-disk encryption, use MFA on all corporate accounts, and join meetings from private spaces when discussing confidential matters.

## Stipend
Approved fully remote employees receive a $75 monthly home-office stipend. Hybrid employees are not eligible for the stipend.
