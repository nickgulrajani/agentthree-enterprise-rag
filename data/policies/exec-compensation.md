# Executive Compensation Guidelines

## Bonus Targets
EVP-level annual bonus targets range from 40% to 60% of base salary, subject to board approval and company performance gates.

## Equity
Long-term incentive grants for executives use a 4-year vest with a 1-year cliff. Change-of-control acceleration requires Compensation Committee approval.

## Confidentiality
Compensation band details are restricted. Unauthorized disclosure is grounds for disciplinary action.
