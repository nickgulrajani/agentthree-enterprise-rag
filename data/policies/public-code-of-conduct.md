# Code of Conduct

## Respect
We treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect. Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation are prohibited.

## Reporting
Anyone may report concerns to ethics@example.com or anonymously via the ethics hotline. Retaliation against good-faith reporters is a terminable offense.

## Integrity
Do not misrepresent company capabilities, fabricate data, or share confidential information outside authorized channels.
